Terms and Privacy

This is a personal, non-commercial app used by one person, its developer, to keep a private copy of their own health records. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it does

With the patient's permission, it reads the patient's own records (read-only) from their health system's patient API and stores them on a private server the developer controls. It never writes to, changes or deletes anything in the health system's records.

Data

Records are stored encrypted and are not sold, shared or given to any third party. No advertising, analytics or tracking is used. This website itself stores nothing and receives only the one-time sign-in code, which is useless on its own and expires within minutes.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the patient portal (in MyChart: Account Settings, then Linked Apps and Devices, or similar), after which the app can no longer read anything.

No warranty

Provided as is, with no warranty. It is not medical advice and not a medical device.
